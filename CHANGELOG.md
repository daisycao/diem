# Changelog

## 1.0.0 · 2026-10-11

First public release.

- The paper, the pond and the timeline, in English and Chinese (follows your system language).
- Import a handwritten page: drop a photo on the window, then say "Import my paper" in Claude Code. Snap it again at night and ticks become done.
- Write to Calendar: timeline blocks go to a "Diem" calendar, so your iPhone shows them with alerts.
- Daily review written by Toto, starting from your paper: what got done, planned vs done, where the time went, tomorrow. It learns from what you tell it.
- Meet the crew: Pip, Toto, Fetch, Zaza.
- Labs (off by default): One brain, Zaza alerts, Places, almanac colors.
